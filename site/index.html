The checker can't be talked into agreeing

Enforced in code, not asked for in a prompt.

The verifier

Sees the video, the brand card and the flight plan. Never the Director's notes, so it has to measure.

The goal re-check

Sees only your mission and three frames. Never the checklist, so it catches a video that passes every check and misses the point.

Two model families

The builder runs on DeepSeek. The checkers run on NVIDIA Nemotron, so they don't share its blind spots.
